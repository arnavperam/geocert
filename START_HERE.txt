WHAT WAS BUILT AND RUN
- core/budget_counts.py: exact targeted gene-owner enumeration, minimum cost for
  every attainable count, hard-budget intervals for every delta, Lagrangian outer
  bounds with exact integer rounding, boundary witnesses and separate gaps.
- core/optimal_cuts.py: the standard Picard-Queyranne residual DAG for ALL optimal
  penalized cuts, projection to owner bits, possible owners, bounded enumeration.
- core/calibration.py: a split-conformal quantile utility with abstention and
  calibration/evaluation identifier checks. No biological calibration was fitted.
- IntegerContrast: signed integer unary count contrasts using the same algorithms.
  Robust contrast signs do not establish statistical differential expression/FDR.
- geocert_counts.py: executable native-model query and deterministic replay API.
- experiments/budget_census.py: complete same-data native analysis with a sound
  singleton-threshold enclosure as comparator, including every null result.
- experiments/perturb_map_diagnostic.py: executed synthetic demonstration that
  local Gumbel perturbations plus exact MAP need not sample the exact Gibbs law.

AN IMPORTANT NEGATIVE THEORETICAL RESULT
Two RNA sites have minimum costs at counts 0, 1, 2 of 0, 1/16, 7/64.
At delta=3/50, the exact interval is [0,0], but the BEST integer-rounded dual
interval is [0,1]. At delta=1/16, count one is budget-optimal but never wins any
penalty. The dual is sound; exactness requires witness agreement or another proof.

WHAT REMAINS UNBUILT OR UNPROVED
- Learned/calibrated geometric families and realistic error budgets.
- New donors, tissue/platform breadth, orthogonal transcript ownership labels.
- Held-out interval coverage, error AUROC, statistical DE/FDR or LR conclusions.
- Matched ground-truth comparisons to Proseg, Baysor, ResolVI, BIDCell, Segger,
  ovrlpy; they have different output objects and need task-specific comparisons.
- General planar or 3D geometry, a geometry-specific hardness proof, global
  relaxation/stitching guarantees, Rust/C++, dynamic flows or whole-slide scaling.
- A licensed pip/conda distribution, SpatialData import, napari/Explorer overlays.
- A published biological claim overturned or rescued by independent evidence.

CLAIM BOUNDARIES
The cost is minimum deformation for a complete owner vector. The new targeted
enumeration additionally minimizes over all assignments with a specified count.
Only the released observed-gene vocabulary is covered; unknown genes are rejected
rather than treated as unmeasured zeros. All counts condition on a corridor and
its settings; outside assignments stay
fixed. Overlapping local corridors are not a coherent whole-tissue family.
Delta is an area budget. Mu is an optimization dual variable, not a learned prior
rate. Deterministic intervals have no nominal probability without calibration.
AI assisted software, analysis and writing; human proof review and full authorship
responsibility remain necessary. No software license was chosen on the author's
behalf. Do not claim historical firstness or guaranteed acceptance.

PAIRED-VALIDATION UPDATE
Run python tests/test_paired_validation.py. The harness calibrates actual-boundary
area on disjoint units and measures family containment separately from count
coverage. Nominal synthetic count coverage is 60/60 with family containment
51/60; an out-of-family evaluation shift gives 60/60 versus 41/60. Five invalid
calibration units cause abstention. These are software fixtures, not real donors.
