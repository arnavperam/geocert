# Response to the consolidation review

**Choice of paper.** Retained Atlas, its compatible global family and exact count-chain algorithm. Adopted the other revision's MIT license, supplied Proseg records and hard-capacity clarification. Did not adopt its narrower title or replace the DP with exponential target enumeration. PR #3 remains a distinct unmerged source revision.

**Family reach.** Completed all four frozen physical support widths on both input collections. The displacement allowance reaches about 2.23 µm, and the latest endpoint thresholds reach 4.88 µm² in lung and 13.78 µm² in breast. The trade-off is substantial coverage loss. Aggregate Proseg and nuclear-reference count reach decreases. This is evidence of a remaining model limitation, not an improvement to hide behind larger displacement numbers.

**Saturation.** Reported every original and fine-grid budget. Constant non-singleton totals do not prove endpoint saturation: 61 breast intervals widen between 0.25 and 0.5, and three between 0.5 and 1. The corrected paper distinguishes occupancy, width and endpoint thresholds.

**Denominators.** The primary 4/46 lung and 203/1,026 breast question results and 139/467 affected breast cells reproduce. Original row totals and nulls remain. New fine-grid outcomes are separately labelled; no denominator is switched to inflate the original result.

**Theorem/application alignment.** Implemented the proposed fallback: a complete one-interface proof in the main text, and the full coupled ordered-boundary proof in the supplement. The atlas application no longer appears to test a thickness-coupling advantage it does not use. Three-label ribbons were not added without validation.

**Proseg.** Reused the actual supplied 3.2.0 runs; independently reconstructed transcript joins and both output count matrices. Added a reach/coverage comparison against these outputs on fixed keys across all widths. The no-factorization adaptation, one-run scope, omitted observations and high background rates are explicit. No new five-run or accuracy study is claimed.

**Real publication data.** The available breast data remain the vendor's trimmed format-test input. It has not been renamed or swapped for an unacquired dataset. The package includes a prospective Janesick/additional-lung acquisition workflow, fixed crop selection, halo handling and five stochastic Proseg runs with failures retained. It was not dispatched, and does not contribute results.

**Presentation.** Removed duplicate page 1; added observed transcripts, scale bar and legend to the tissue figure; inserted the complete width/coverage result; retained the exact LDHB witness. The main text distinguishes an analyst's count sensitivity from corrected ownership. The supplement holds the full budget census, general theorem and adapted-Proseg details. The author briefing gives worked examples of gluing, critical heights and the hard-budget DP.

**Release.** MIT license and source-data attribution are included. Version 1.1.0 is a local publication candidate. Compressed large records have expanded-byte checksums and an offline restore command. The proposed CI checks table hashes and reruns exact computations. Current GitHub tools allow reads but no publication, so no tag, new commit or Atlas CI success is invented. `release/PUBLISH.md` gives the remaining non-destructive publication steps and availability edit.

**Submission judgment.** The package is materially stronger as a bounded, self-contained methods paper. The reach experiment does not eliminate the concern that the uncertainty family misses realistic segmentation disagreements. Scientific novelty and usefulness remain peer-review risks; neither review agreement nor test counts guarantees acceptance.
