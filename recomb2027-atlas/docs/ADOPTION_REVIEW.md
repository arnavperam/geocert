# Consolidation decision

The manuscript remains **Certified Expression Intervals under Shared Segmentation Uncertainty**. The reference partition, globally compatible atlas and exact count-chain algorithm are retained. The 0.2.0a2 corridor paper is not substituted for them.

## Adopted from 0.2.0a2

- MIT software license and its existing Arnav Peram copyright notice. Source-data CC BY 4.0 terms are separate.
- Saved Proseg 3.2.0 inputs, outputs, failed initial run, exclusions and commands. A new checker joins transcript IDs, checks observed coordinates and source owners, maps output cells through original IDs, and reconstructs the output count matrix.
- Explicit distinction between logical hard arcs and the numerical hard capacity chosen after unary costs and the area multiplier are specified.
- Clear disclosure of source-admissibility for closure shortcuts and of AI assistance.

The other manuscript's 80/652 pair census and 22/85 infeasibilities were already present in the research record. The budget-one 58-versus-40 admission comparison is narrower new evidence. Neither replaces global cell-count composition. Its Proseg run is an adapted, one-run-per-crop execution diagnostic; no held-out accuracy claim or five-run replication is imported.

- Explicit homeomorphism and area conversion for disjoint ribbons.
- One common uncertainty family and area budget for each crop, with complete retained counts for eligible cells.
- The LDHB example and all null baseline comparisons.

## Feedback checked rather than copied

The denominator audit reproduces 4/46 tightened eligible lung questions and 203/1,026 breast questions, with 139/467 breast cells affected. These describe questions with a marginal width of at least two at some original tested budget, not independently replicated biological effects.

The number of non-singleton intervals is constant from area 0.25 onward. This does not imply that every endpoint is constant. In the original breast tables, 61 intervals change between 0.25 and 0.5 and three change between 0.5 and 1. The largest exact endpoint-saturation thresholds are about 0.240525 and 0.573939 square micrometres for lung and breast respectively. The revised analysis reports non-singleton occupancy, summed width and endpoint changes separately.

The primary application uses one interface per ribbon. Its proof and count algorithm are presented directly in the main text; the complete coupled-interface extension is retained in the supplement. Native thickness benefit is not inferred from the scalar evaluation.

## What was not imported as evidence

Simulated reviewer votes, acceptance probabilities, claims of perfection, unexecuted external acquisition, a new donor, and a Proseg accuracy advantage are not results. The public 0.2.0a2 CI passed; this does not establish that the newly consolidated Atlas release has run on GitHub.
