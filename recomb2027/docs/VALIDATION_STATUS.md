## Ground-truth design cautions

A reference label must be defined independently of the method under evaluation.
Mutually exclusive markers measure a biological proxy, not known transcript
ownership. XIST is not a universal female-only ground-truth marker: somatic
activation in male human cancers is documented (Sadagopan et al., Cell Systems
13:932--944.e5,2022, doi:10.1016/j.cels.2022.10.002). Species identity or reporter
identity also requires an assay-specific error model and aligned cells; neither
by itself validates all membrane locations or excludes extracellular signal.
No new biological material was generated or acquired in this task.

## What an external evaluation would require

Use donor-level disjoint training/calibration/test material, aligned cell identity,
independently adjudicated membrane and transcript-owner references, retained
failed/topology-incompatible cases, and a frozen extraction/parameter/query
protocol. Compare every method on the same measured molecules and label mapping.
Report donor-level or independent-region replication rather than treating repeated
budgets or thousands of correlated transcripts as independent experiments.

The included evaluation module accepts standardized ALIGNED reference/prediction
records and count intervals. It refuses missing nonzero truth entries and repeated
transcript IDs. It does not run, tune or impersonate other segmentation software.
A probability method and a set-valued certificate answer different questions;
interval coverage/width and assignment discrimination must be compared separately.
