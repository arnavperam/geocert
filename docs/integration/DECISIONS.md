# Integration decisions

The Atlas implementation remains the scientific base. Its disjoint-patch homeomorphisms and exact scalar count chain solve problems that the alternative corridor-local release explicitly leaves open. We adopt that release's MIT license, Proseg 3.2.0 execution records, input exclusions and cell mapping, and its precise distinction between hard logical arcs and objective-dependent finite capacities.

The pair census of 80 baseline and 652 enlarged questions is an earlier result, not a newly discovered advance. The new 58-versus-40 baseline admission comparison is correctly scoped to pinned assignments; it does not replace the atlas hard-budget calculation.

The review's non-singleton saturation numbers reproduce, but its inference that the budget never binds above 0.25 is too strong: 61 breast-test intervals still change after that budget. We report both indicator and endpoint saturation.

The original useful-denominator census reproduces: 4/46 lung and 203/1,026 breast-test cell–gene questions with marginal width at least two are tightened on the original eight-budget grid. Affected cells are 4/153 and 139/467. New fine-grid and wider-family denominators are reported separately.

The main paper states the one-interface theorem that the atlas actually uses. The general coupled-interface proof remains in the supplement. No three-label atlas benefit, biological ownership truth, new Proseg execution, calibrated uncertainty or new independent donor is asserted.

The title remains Certified Expression Intervals under Shared Segmentation Uncertainty. Registration or submission is an author action; no submission confirmation was supplied or generated.
