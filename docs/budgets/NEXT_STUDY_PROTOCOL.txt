NEXT STUDY DESIGN - DRAFT TO FREEZE BEFORE NEW OUTCOME INSPECTION

Primary target
Conditional observed-count intervals for a declared geometric error family,
followed by a separately justified calibration/coverage claim. Primary comparison:
interval width at matched empirical coverage against sound singleton enclosures.
The calibration target, exclusion policy and baseline outputs must be fixed first.

Sampling and separation
Use donor/slide identifiers as independent units. Keep training, calibration and
evaluation donors disjoint. Overlapping corridors, gene queries and penalties are
dependent outputs, not biological replicates. Record all candidate regions and
reasons for rejection, including non-ordered topology. Report retained coverage of
the tissue. Do not turn post-hoc selected corridors into a claimed held-out cohort.

Matched geometry
Prefer registered measurements of the same cells using independently delineated
membranes or expert annotations. Serial sections can change cells and geometry;
they require a correspondence model and are not interchangeable paired truth.
Fit slope/band/endpoint rules on training data. Define a nested uncertainty family
and a score for the least family member containing the reference geometry. Treat
out-of-family geometry as a failed containment, not as a discarded easy case.
Area budgets alone do not correct misspecified slopes, topology or nuclear pins.

Conformal budget option
With a frozen valid nonconformity score and exchangeable calibration units, use
rank ceil((n+1)*(1-alpha)). A nonfinite threshold triggers abstention. At alpha=.05,
fewer than 19 calibration units cannot yield a finite distribution-free threshold
by this standard unrandomized order statistic. That is not a power calculation.
If simultaneous within-donor protection is wanted, define the donor score using
the relevant maximum before calibration. A donor-level marginal guarantee does
not establish conditional coverage for every cell type, gene or platform.
The supplied utility only computes this quantile; no biological score was fitted.

Ground-truth hierarchy and controls
1. Mosaics/reporters: validate probes and independent cell/clone identification.
   Reporter transcripts provide partial labels; cells sharing a reporter remain
   indistinguishable. Control extracellular material and transfer/phagocytosis.
2. Sex-mismatched material: independently establish donor/host identity. Control
   sex-chromosome changes, dropout and atypical expression. XIST is documented in
   male cancers and is not an automatic error label. Sex labels do not distinguish
   individual same-sex cells.
3. Xenografts: validate species specificity and hybrid-reference ambiguity with
   controls. Cross-species labels are partial ownership labels. Same-species cell
   swaps remain invisible. Account for ambient and engulfed RNA.
4. Simulation: start from realistic masks/3D geometry and a declared transcript
   generation model, with true owners retained. Keep simulation fitting separate
   from evaluation. Ordered-corridor toy tests in this release are not that study.
5. Mutually exclusive markers: choose panels from independent evidence and report
   this as a proxy. Genuine co-expression must not be deleted by the evaluation's
   definition of truth. Do not use the same marker rule to define and validate cells.

Baseline tasks
- Sound single-RNA thresholds: already implemented under the same constraints.
- Distance to boundary: error-ranking baseline; distance alone is not a certified
  area-cost bound without regularity/clearance assumptions.
- Proseg/Baysor/BIDCell/Segger: segmentation/assignment outputs, matched inputs and
  provenance; assess accuracy on available labels rather than pretending they
  natively return the same deterministic budget interval.
- ResolVI: probabilistic correction/representation; compare appropriate counts,
  ranking and calibration endpoints with identical test labels and splits.
- ovrlpy: vertical-overlap diagnostic; evaluate its intended task separately.
Pin releases, parameters, data accessions, compute budgets and failure policies.

Metrics and replication
Report interval coverage AND width versus budget; measure actual family coverage.
Report empirical failures when geometry lies outside the family. AUROC/AUPRC need
credible positive and negative labels and a declared population. Include singleton
and distance baselines. Confidence intervals must reflect donor/slide dependence;
the current one-donor results do not support biological replication intervals.
Statistical DE/FDR requires a separate test with biological replicates and a
specified multiple-testing procedure. Robust linear-count signs are only a
descriptive intermediate result. For co-expression or LR analyses, distinguish
joint attainability, detection, biological expression and actual signaling.

Interpretation of a published biological claim
Select claims and endpoints before looking at favorable results. A feasible
boundary that reproduces an expression pattern shows model-conditioned fragility,
not that segmentation error caused the published observation. A preserved count
under a calibrated family is evidence of robustness to that family, not proof of
the underlying cell identity or biological mechanism.

Engineering acceptance gates
Define a coherent full-tissue uncertainty family before claiming local stitching.
Prove projection containment and cost accounting for any relaxation. Preserve
molecule identity, shared boundaries and global budget allocation. Test sparse
matrix interchange on small validated examples before SpatialData/napari wrappers.
Benchmark rational bit lengths, compiler cost, memory, failures and query reuse.
A fast C++ max-flow alone does not give general geometry or million-cell capacity.
