# Merged Atlas candidate 0.3.0a2

## Decision

Use **Certified Expression Intervals under Shared Segmentation Uncertainty** as the scientific paper. This supersedes the corridor-only registration recommendation. Atlas supplies a compatible fixed-seam partition and polynomial scalar hard-budget spectra; the corridor revision supplies actual Proseg execution, licensing and release infrastructure. No registration or conference submission has been performed.

## Verified and adopted

- Original 210,240 intervals and 26,280 complete spectra reproduced exactly from prepared inputs.
- Retained compatible disjoint-patch gluing, constant physical Jacobian and scalar critical-height/count algorithm. General coupled-interface theory is stated separately from the scalar Atlas application.
- Restored both effect denominators:203/1,026 breast multi-move questions and203/22,976 overall;4/46 lung multi-move questions and4/3,304 overall.139/467 breast cells have at least one tightened question.
- Corrected saturation: lung endpoints plateau at 0.25 and breast at 1 square micrometre on the evaluated grid. Constant non-singleton counts at 0.25 do not imply constant endpoints.
- Corrected provenance: earlier pair-census totals were already in the original supplement. Their replay is verification, not a newly discovered scientific result.
- Added mandatory atlas/source/observation/geometry-audit binding at certification entry, with 17 deliberate invalid cases rejected.
- Fixed a real count-chain API bug outside the declared point-input contract. Unsupported free horizontal location intervals and multisite groups are rejected; 7 unsupported cases and 3 supported point quantifier modes are tested. Native tables are unchanged.
- Integrated actual Proseg 3.2.0 outputs on both lung crops, including failed default factorization, adapted no-factorization configuration, exclusions, source hashes and raw outputs.
- Added a frozen post-review lung width/reach study. Shared observed populations remain fixed across widths; Proseg diffusion coordinates are not substituted for measured positions. Exact witness checks passed for 8,270 local outcomes and 25,610 global state traces.
- Corrected page numbering, added transcripts/legend/10-micrometre scale bar to the tissue figure, added a reach figure, preserved self-contained mathematics and format-test disclosure.
- MIT-licensed code and public review branch, with a complete archived reproducibility package.

These values are band-implied upper bounds before slope and nuclear restrictions, not proven attained displacements. Halfwidth 12 occurs in 132/544 patches; halfwidth 28 in 41/543. Other accepted halfwidths are at most 4. The cap 12 and cap 28 width ladders are different and the greedy atlases are re-extracted, so the families are not nested. The largest cap reduces reach. These descriptive results do not establish realistic segmentation uncertainty or accuracy. The nuclear denominator differs from the earlier 1128-key report because the earlier analysis dropped 6 gene queries with mutable molecules outside the 5-micrometre comparison guard. The revised analysis gives those excluded molecules zero score while retaining them as geometric constraints; the exact six-key discrepancy audit is retained.
