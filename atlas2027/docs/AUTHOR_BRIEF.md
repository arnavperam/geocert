# What the author must be able to defend

**The object.** For every complete feasible transcript assignment, the original graph represents the exact minimum continuous boundary deformation. This is stronger than solving a finite list of penalties. Its ingredients include established envelopes, lattice closure and graph gadgets; its precise continuous cost-preservation claim is the result to compare with prior work.

**Simultaneous attainment.** The propagated lower and upper envelope families and the reference satisfy the same difference constraints. Coordinatewise minimum and maximum preserve those constraints. Clamping the reference between these envelopes is therefore jointly feasible and pointwise nearest, not merely a list of independently optimal coordinates.

**The new hard-budget algorithm.** A canonical minimizer for every assignment lives on the complete critical mesh. At a mesh node, an admissible height determines the owner of each point RNA there. A height/count shortest path preserves the exact minimum for each count. The count dimension is polynomial for explicitly listed unit molecules. It is not a claim about every multi-boundary or free-interval model.

**Why the global count is well defined.** Patch supports are disjoint and their boundary traces are fixed. Each patch has an explicit homeomorphism, so their changes glue into one partition. Every editable RNA belongs to one patch. Fixed contributions are counted once and one area budget is shared. Marginal endpoints of different matrix entries need not occur together.

**What the atlas excludes.** Uncovered boundaries, seams, junction neighborhoods, crop-border cells and nuclear owners remain fixed. Approximately two-thirds of incident reference boundary length is editable. These are not calibrated realistic-error sets.

**Which comparison says what.** A marginal outer bound discards shared feasibility/budget; an additive inner model overcharges shared deformation. The nuclear-expansion reference asks whether another deterministic segmentation lies in the family. It is not Proseg or biological truth. Countwise inclusion does not prove that a full alternative matrix is attainable.

**Data denominators.** Lung: 153 complete reference cells, 544 patches, 7,370 retained RNAs, 745 editable RNAs, 3,304 cell/gene spectra. Breast: 467 complete cells, 1,708 patches, 81,182 retained RNAs, 8,262 editable RNAs, 22,976 spectra. The breast source is explicitly vendor format-test data, not biological validation. Neither query counts nor patch counts are independent biological replicates.

**The adverse evidence.** Marginal bounds already match most endpoints: 4 lung and 228 breast rows improve. The additive inner model misses 2 lung and 68 breast endpoints. The distinct nuclear-expansion reference is often outside the atlas, and its full assignment is infeasible in all four crops. The method's correctness does not turn these into positive calibration results.

**Disclosure and release.** AI contributed to mathematical development, algorithms, implementation, experiments, checks and writing. Approval, defense and responsibility remain with the author. No acceptance probability is established, and the merged candidate is available on its public review branch; conference submission has not been performed. Do not silently change an already registered title or materially change the abstract.


**Model identity is checked before certification.** Prepared atlas content, original source bytes, fixed counts, editable transcript membership and the prior rational geometry audit must agree. This is reproducibility validation, not a cryptographic guarantee against forged audit records. New atlases must pass the rational geometry check before certification.

**Point-input contract matters.** The count chain accepts free groups consisting of true point locations with separated owner thresholds. A horizontal location interval can give overlapping owner possibilities; the scalar point chain is then inapplicable. The merged code rejects that case instead of labeling its output exact. Structural constraints may still occupy intervals under their stated contract.
