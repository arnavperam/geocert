COMPARISON AND MERGE DECISION

Use the Atlas mathematical construction as the new paper's base. It supplies a single compatible fixed-seam partition family and a polynomial exact hard-budget algorithm for explicitly represented point molecules on scalar patches. Those are substantive extensions beyond the corridor-only revision's identity safeguards and targeted exponential enumeration.

Retain the corridor revision's reproducible release practices, MIT software licensing, actual Proseg outputs and alignment checks, and precise separation of conditional guarantees from biological evidence. Keep the complete general ownerwise theorem, while stating that all evaluated atlas patches use its scalar specialization. Multi-boundary thickness coupling is a broader result, not an explanation of the Atlas experiment's improvements.

Two corrections to the supplied evaluation are necessary. First, the pair-census totals 80/652 and 22/85 were already in the earlier supplement. The latest corridor revision independently checked those results and added budget comparisons; the totals should not be described as a new discovery. Second, an unchanged number of non-singleton intervals does not show budget nonbinding. Breast endpoints change for 61 questions between 0.25 and 0.5, and for 3 between 0.5 and 1. The sampled plateau is 0.25 for lung endpoints and 1 for breast endpoints.

Report both all-row and opportunity-specific denominators. The marginal-width-at-least-two definition identifies 46 lung and 1026 breast cell-gene questions; exact geometry tightens 4 and 203 at some tested budget, respectively. The breast numerator affects 139 of 467 reference cells. These are correlated computational questions, not independent biological replicates or accuracy rates.

The main unresolved restriction is physical reach. Metadata give a pixel size near 0.212500013 um. The original chart band implies a Euclidean boundary displacement at most 0.450781 um before nuclear and slope restrictions. Width sensitivity is a diagnostic of how geometry and coverage change; it does not calibrate boundaries to anatomy. The breast inputs remain vendor format-test data and retain that label in the abstract and results.

The merged paper is therefore stronger as an exact-methods candidate, but is not independently biologically validated. A second real cohort with appropriate specimen-level splits and ownership or membrane references remains new experimental work.

The executed post-review width study re-extracted lung atlases at largest accepted band-implied displacement caps 0.451, 1.352 and 3.155 um. These are upper bounds before slope and nuclear restrictions, rather than optimized attained displacements. The enlarged halfwidths occur in only 132/544 and 41/543 patches, respectively; other accepted halfwidths remain at most 4. Unlimited-area reach of changed nuclear counts is 186/1134, 223/1134 and 190/1134; Proseg reach is 185/2798, 241/2798 and 200/2798. Greedy reconstruction and different fallback ladders make the families non-nested, so this is sensitivity across rebuilt families rather than monotone enlargement. The best tested settings still reach only 19.7% and 8.6% of the respective changed-count populations. The default nuclear denominator differs from the earlier 185/1128 because six previously excluded boundary-gene questions are retained with exact zero-score treatment of excluded molecules; one is newly reachable, and all previously shared questions agree.
