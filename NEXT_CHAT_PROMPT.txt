IMPORTANT CORRECTIONS TO THE DREAM PROPOSAL
- Theorem 3 supports penalized unary objectives. It alone does not make arbitrary
  hard-budget optima one-cut solvable. There is an executed strict duality gap.
- GGT nesting requires monotonicity conditions; it does not follow for all count
  objectives. Current path code uses repeated exact cuts.
- Picard-Queyranne is established theory. Its DAG covers all penalized optima,
  not all feasible continuous boundaries or all hard-budget assignments.
- Profiling boundaries into D*(c) is not marginalizing a continuous prior.
  Low-order perturb-and-MAP is generally not exact Gibbs sampling; a diagnostic
  here measures a clear discrepancy on a two-state geometric example.
- Planar multiway cut with fixed terminal count is polynomial. A general
  nonplanar three-label hardness result is not a reduction for this geometry.
- Tree adjacency, star-shaped cells and 3D surfaces have not been solved here.
- TISSUE and other uncertainty-aware spatial methods exist; priority is unproved.
- XIST can be expressed in male cancers. Species/reporter labels require controls
  and generally do not identify the exact owner among same-species/same-clone cells.
- Robust count/contrast bounds are not statistical DE significance, FDR control,
  proof of cell identity, or evidence of ligand-receptor signaling.
